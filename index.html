I am a professional English–Spanish interpreter and a trained executive assistant. As an interpreter, I carefully choose my words so the message has the closest communicative impact to the original on the other party, with the composure that live, high-stakes calls demand. As an executive assistant, I bring new ideas to make work run faster, with an AI-native way of working with Claude Code, GoHighLevel and ClickUp. Creativity and resourcefulness are what set my work apart. Let me guide you through my profile and show you why I am a fit for both.

Why me as your interpreter

01

Calm when it matters most

Emergency and 911 calls, emergency rooms and labor and delivery, where I supported 35+ births as a consecutive interpreter. Composure is part of the job.

02

I guide the call

Interpreting is more than rendering words. I manage turns, ask for clarification when something is unclear and keep everyone on track, so the call reaches its goal.

03

When things go sideways

Bad audio, people talking over each other, a dropped line, a frustrated caller: real calls rarely go to plan. I stay resourceful and find a way to keep communication going.

04

Listening beyond the words

From hospice and mental health to victim services, I listen closely and adapt my tone, so people feel heard in both languages.

05

Every word counts

Informed consent, recorded statements and witness testimonies: calls where every word carries legal weight, so details are never optional.

06

Bridging cultures, not just languages

Some things are hard to interpret not because of terminology but because of culture. I make sure the meaning lands, not only the words.

07

Range across fields

Medical and insurance calls every day; legal, immigration, banking, customer service and education every week, by phone and video.

08

Two years you can rely on

Two years of live interpreting, with more than 75,000 minutes in 2026 alone. Medical calls every day mean private health information is part of my routine, handled with HIPAA compliance on every call.

09

Rated by the people I help

Clients rate my calls 4.9 out of 5 on average, and more than 500 of them have given five stars.

International by practice

Calm when plans change

Phone work comes naturally
