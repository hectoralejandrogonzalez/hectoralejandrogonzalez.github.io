What I bring

Eight qualities I use every day, led by creativity, and where I learned each one: live interpreting calls, a sales floor and a busy restaurant counter.

01

Creativity

I am a creative person, whatever the job. When interpreting, what I enjoy most is what makes me think: finding a creative rendition so the message lands with the same effect, interpreting a joke or an expression, or letting one of the parties know that we need a different kind of solution, because that part of the message does not carry the same value in the other language, whether because of its meaning or its grammar. Talent, practice and knowledge matter, but creativity is key to solving many of the difficulties that come up. I am constantly coming up with new ideas and solutions to be more efficient and make things work faster, and I design and build my own projects, from this website to apps, games and workflows.

02

Emotional intelligence

Comes from interpreting in hospice and palliative care, mental health sessions, victim services and domestic violence cases. In those calls I learned to read how people feel, keep my tone steady and make room for hard news.

03

Active listening

Consecutive interpreting means hearing the whole message before I say a word. Attorney-client calls, therapy sessions and witness statements taught me to catch the meaning, the tone and what is left unsaid.

04

Resourcefulness

At Burger King, as a team member and a team lead, I learned to solve whatever came up during a shift with what was at hand. At Solvo, I researched the options for each customer and built rapport, so they could see for themselves that it was the best choice before closing each sale.

05

Proactiveness

I like to stay one step ahead. I think of possible solutions before something becomes a problem, I always do something extra, and if I see that something is wrong, I let you know right away. I keep learning and improving, too.

06

Composure under pressure

I interpret in emergency rooms, operating rooms and labor and delivery, and I have supported 35+ births. In those calls, staying calm is part of the job, and I bring that same calm to any deadline.

07

Detail-oriented

I double-check everything before I say it or send it. When I give an answer or finish a task, I’m sure it’s right. Interpreting taught me this: one wrong word can change the whole message.

08

Teamwork

At Burger King, I worked as a team member and a team lead, influencing and inspiring my coworkers so every shift ran at its best quality. As an interpreter, I work alongside doctors, nurses, attorneys and agents so each call gets where it needs to go.
